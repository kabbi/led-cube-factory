# Triangle on the 30 mm edge: shallow connector, 3535 LEDs

2026-10-16. Result: `tri30-3535x10-p5-shallow-pvia` (and its pitch twin
`sq30-3535x16-p5-pvia`).

**Question.** A triangle that mates with the 30 mm squares, with 4 LEDs on
the long side (4+3+2+1) or 8 per side.

## What doesn't fit

The standard `edge_conn` pads reach 1.385 mm in from the edge, and the
0.8 mm hole (0.35-1.15 mm in, plus a 0.15 mm ring) alone forces ~1.3 mm of
depth. On a 30 mm triangle that leaves:

| LEDs | pitch needed | pitch the parts need | smallest edge that works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 x 5050 per side | ~4.3 mm | >= 6.1 mm (bodies collide) | ~35-36 mm |
| 8 x 2020 per side | ~2.7 mm | >= 3.1 mm | ~33-35 mm |

The 4 x 5050 doesn't fit even with zero-depth pads: the body alone uses the
whole margin. So: 3535 (WS2812B-Mini), and a shallower connector.

## Shallow connector

The hole is there to avoid castellated edges (expensive at JLC), so it
stays, just smaller: `edge_conn_shallow` has two 0.3 mm plated holes per pad
(JLC's standard via drill), 1.2 mm apart, starting 0.38 mm from the edge.
Pads reach 0.95 mm in instead of 1.385 mm. Pad positions, widths and pinout
are the same as `edge_conn`, so triangles still mate with the squares.

3535 at 0 deg, before and after (geometry check, not DRC):

| pad depth | pitch | gap between LEDs | gap to connector pads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.385 mm | 5.2 mm | 0.42 mm | 0.06 mm |
| 0.95 mm | 5.3 mm | 0.52 mm | 0.41 mm (0.75 mm measured: the pads have gaps) |

## Inner-pad 3535 footprint

Same trick as the 5050: `LED_WS2812B-Mini_PLCC4_3.5x3.5mm_InnerPads` moves
the pad centres from x = +-1.64 to +-0.95 (outer edge 0.05 mm inside the
body), courtyard body + 0.25. At 5.3 mm pitch: copper between LEDs 0.52 ->
1.90 mm, LED pads to connector pads 0.75 -> 1.25 mm. After that the plastic
bodies, not copper, set the limits. (Check against the Worldsemi drawing /
JLC preview before the first order.)

## Matching the squares' pitch

| pitch | DRC | LED pads -> connector pads | LED body -> connector pads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.3 mm | clean | 1.25 mm | 0.77 mm |
| 5.8 mm | clean | 0.64 mm | 0.30 mm |
| 6.0 mm | clean | 0.47 mm | 0.12 mm |
| 6.2 mm | clean | 0.30 mm | 0.00 (body on the pads) |
| 7.112 mm (sq 5050 pitch) | 42 errors | overlapping | overlapping |

DRC only checks copper, so 6.2 passes while unusable. Settled on 5.0 mm and
made a square at the same pitch instead. To go wider, see
[LED rotation](2026-10-16-led-rotation.md).

## The square at 5 mm can't use the hand-drawn backs

Template vias land on LED pads on a 5 mm grid:

- `square30`, 4x4: the WS_OUT hand-off via sits on D13's GND pad.
- `square30-dense`, 4x4: same, at D6's VDD pad.
- 5x5 on either: routes, but 8-10 DRC errors where template vias short to
  LED pads.

So `sq30-3535x16-p5-pvia` has a generated back (26/32 patterned vias).

## Triangle back

Three-wing hubs (one jumper per port), PASS in the top corner, IN/OUT in the
lower ones. Hub circle 5.5 mm, `jumper_radius` 3.0: the three inner wings
meet near the centre, so `radius - jumper_radius >= 2.4` keeps their
courtyards apart (the first layout overlapped). One cap per edge: two per
edge collide in the corners. 14/20 power vias patterned.

Side change for all generated backs: hubs moved from a 6.0 to a 7.0 mm
circle (IN-OUT pads 1.49 -> 3.22 mm), which then needed a via keep-out on
the hub labels and port numbers moved 2.5 mm along the edge.
