# Dense 2020 square + triangle pair

2026-10-16. Result: `tri30-2020x36-p2.95-shallow-pvia` +
`sq30-2020x64-p2.95-pvia` (8 per side), and `tri30-2020x28-p3.4-shallow-pvia`
(7 rows, matching the template `sq30-2020x64-p3.4-tpl`).

**Question.** 2020 squares and triangles at a matching pitch, as many LEDs
as the triangle allows, then the same LEDs per side.

## Sizing

The triangle sets the numbers. A 2020's pads reach 1.55 mm towards a slanted
edge (more than its body), so 8 rows fit up to ~2.97 mm pitch with 0.15 mm
to the shallow connector pads; 2.95 mm leaves 0.2 mm. (A 9x9 square also fits
at that pitch; dropped to 8x8 to match LEDs per side.) Seven rows at 3.4 mm
leave 0.28 mm.

## What it took to route

- **Pad gaps.** Neighbouring LEDs' pads are 0.42 mm apart and DOUT/DIN sit
  diagonally opposite, so in-row links squeeze between the GND and VDD pads:
  `rules: {track_width: 0.127, clearance: 0.1}`.
- **Via holes.** Vias keep `hole_copper_clearance` (was hard-coded 0.25, now
  a rule) even when `clearance` is finer; the router paints them as bigger
  copper discs (before that fix: 73 DRC errors). At 0.25 the via grid is too
  sparse for the chain's row returns; 0.2 routes. Default boards are
  unaffected (0.15 clearance + 0.1 annular ring = 0.25 already).
- **Courtyard.** The 2020 courtyard is pads + 0.15 mm (was + 0.25, wider than
  the 2.95 mm pitch).
- **Hubs.** The back jumpers displace patterned vias; on the square
  `hubs: {angle: 180}` displaces the fewest (125/128 patterned) and lets P4
  through. Default placement left P4 boxed in.

These rules sit right at JLC's multilayer minimums (0.09/0.09 track/space,
0.2 mm via hole to track, 0.1 mm pad to track), with none of the defaults'
margin: check the DFM report on the first order. Backing off to 3.4 mm
doesn't avoid it: no 3.4 mm triangle routes on default rules either.
