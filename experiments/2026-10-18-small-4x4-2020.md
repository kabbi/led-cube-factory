# Smallest 4x4 2020 tile

2026-10-18. Result: `sq15-2020x16-p3.35-micro-pvia` (first built as
`sq13.5-2020x16-p2.85-mini-pvia`).

**Question.** The smallest 4x4 2020 tile, then the widest pitch on 15 mm.

## 13.5 mm

2.85 mm is the tightest pitch before the LED outlines overlap; at 13.5 mm
the LED pads end 0.25 mm from the connector pads (13.4 mm is the floor).
Needs the fine rules of the [dense 2020 pair](2026-10-16-dense-2020-pair.md).

The hard part is the back: three hubs times four ports need at least two In2
crossings, and a through via has to clear the front too. With the pad rows
0.32 mm apart, vias fit only in the column gaps, and the power vias take
most of those. Most failures left P2 with no path; IN and OUT in opposite
corners next to the first and last LED didn't help. Only one layout routed:
hubs at 180 deg on a 3.4 mm circle, jumper radius 1.7, cap by P2 -- almost no
slack.

Dropping a plane to route on In1 was on the table as a last resort; never
needed.

## 15 mm, then the widest pitch

Sweep: hub angle in 45 deg steps x circle x jumper radius x cap spot.

| edge, pitch | rules | DRC clean | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13.5 mm, 2.85 mm | 0.127/0.1, hole-copper 0.2 | 1 of 144 | pad rows 0.32 mm apart: vias only in the column gaps |
| 15 mm, 2.85 mm | same | 5 of 128 | |
| 15 mm, 3.35 mm | default | 5 of 48 | pad rows 0.82 mm apart: vias fit between rows too |

Widest pitch at 15 mm keeping ~0.25 mm from LED pads to connector pads:
1.5p + 1.265 + 0.25 <= 6.55, p <= 3.357, so **3.35 mm** (0.26 mm). Keeping the
grid even across tile edges would take 3.75 mm, out of reach. Bonus: it
routes on default rules. Final hubs: `angle: 180, radius: 4.2,
jumper_radius: 1.5`, cap by P2.
