# Smallest 2x2 5050 tile

2026-10-18. Result: `sq15-5050x4-p7.5-micro-pvia` (first built as
`sq13-5050x4-p5.5-mini-pvia`).

**Question.** The smallest 2x2 5050 tile, changing the edge connector if
needed.

## 13 mm

At 5.5 mm pitch (LED courtyards touching) the bodies keep 0.3 mm to the
connector pads on a 13 mm square, about what the other tiles keep. Going
smaller would mean shallower pads or overlapping outlines, for ~0.5 mm at
most.

What it took:

- **A smaller connector.** First a "mini" one (shallow depth and pinout,
  1.4 mm pads at 2.2 mm pitch), later replaced by `edge_conn_micro` from the
  [1x1 tile](2026-10-18-small-1x1-5050.md). Neither mates with the 30 mm
  tiles.
- **`jumper: jumper_small`**: 1.45 x 0.8 mm, 0.25 mm gap (solder or a 0402
  0R). Twelve of the 1.3 mm jumpers don't fit on the back.
- **Hubs turned over.** 36 combinations of angle, circle and jumper spacing:
  the default layout boxes in P4; `angle: 180` routes in every combination.
- **`caps.ports`**: one 0603 cap in the free corner by P2, one 100 nF for
  four LEDs.

## 15 mm

Grown to 15 mm to match the 4x4 2020 tile, then the pitch maximised keeping
the 0.3 mm body margin: p/2 + 2.5 + 0.3 <= 6.55 gives exactly **7.5 mm**, half
the edge, so the LED grid stays even across tile edges on a cube. Same hubs
(`angle: 180, radius: 3.0, jumper_radius: 1.6`), default rules.
