# Smallest 1x1 5050 tile: grouped jumpers

2026-10-18. Result: `sq8-5050x1-p5.5-micro-grp-nopass-pvia`, the micro edge
connector, `hubs.layout: grouped`, `hubs.roles`.

**Question.** The smallest single-5050 tile. The front alone would fit
7.5 mm; the back sets the size.

## Micro connector

`edge_conn_micro`: the shallow connector's pinout and depth, pads 0.8 mm wide
at 1.3 mm pitch (6 mm span), one 0.3 mm hole each, connected solid to the
planes (`zone_connect 2`: thermal spokes don't fit the 0.5 mm gaps, so the
pads are harder to solder). The 0.5 mm pad gaps are easy to bridge when
soldering tiles together. Later adopted by the 2x2 and 4x4 tiles too.

## Hub crosses: 12 mm

Three hubs of four jumpers each:

- 10 of 1176 layouts at 9-12 mm DRC clean, all at 12 mm (and only without a
  cap: no cap spot cleared both the hubs and the connector pads).
- With In2 as cheap as the outer layers, 360 more at 9.5-11.5 mm: none. The
  crosses box each other in; a free layer doesn't help.

Options considered to go below 12 mm: hubs on the front (~2.3 mm free ring
around the LED), grouped jumpers by each port, or dropping the PASS hub.

## Grouped, no PASS: 8 mm

`hubs: {layout: grouped, roles: [IN, OUT]}`: instead of a cross of jumpers
around each hub centre, an IN/OUT pair sits by each port (`orient: along`:
lying along the edge, PORT pads in the middle by the connector's port pad),
and each hub net routes as a ring, hopping on the front and In2 where the
rings cross. Silk: `O <port> I` inside each pair. No cap.

| layout | result |
|---|---|
| grouped, jumpers pointing at the edge | 3 clean at 8 mm, 1 at 7.75 mm; below ~7.7 mm adjacent ports' jumpers meet in the corners |
| grouped, jumpers along the edge | 5 clean at 8 mm (spacing 1.8-1.9 x inset 1.5-1.6); none at 7.5-7.75 mm, where the rings find no way around the LED, even with In2 cheap |

Chose 8 mm (a 2x2 block of settings all route) over the single, marginless
7.75 mm hit. Final: `spacing: 1.85, inset: 1.55`.

Without PASS the tile can't pass data through between two neighbours.

On the hub-cross small tiles the back's port numbers keep their 30 mm
offsets, so they land on jumper pads (the mask clips them).
